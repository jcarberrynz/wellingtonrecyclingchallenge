Bonus games

For grown-ups

This game is inspired by the hands-on Wellington Recycling Challenge, where tamariki sort items into the correct bins: e.g Landfill, Plastics, Paper & Cardboard, Glass, Soft Plastics, Lids + Caps and Compost.

  • Wiggly Worms (age 2-3) uses four bins and fewer items. Bin Buddies (age 3-4) uses all eight charts, just like the real mat.
  • Pip's messages appear as text, so younger children will need a grown-up to read along. Kids can drag an item to a bin or just tap the bin. Wrong guesses are gentle: after two tries, the right bin glows.
  • Wellington tips used in the game: take lids off bottles and jars; rinse containers; soft plastics go to supermarket drop-off bins; caps and lids have their own collection scheme; glass goes in its own crate. Batteries never go in any bin — drop them at a library such as Kilbirnie or Karori.
  • At the end of the sorting game, snap a screenshot of the mat, just like the photo step in the real challenge.
  • Each game has its own cheerful tune. Tap 🎵 to turn the music off and 🔊 to turn the sound effects off. On iPhones and iPads, the silent switch also mutes the game.
  • Stickers are saved on this device only. No data is collected.
